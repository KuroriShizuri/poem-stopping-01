A tiny program (Python 3.13, stdlib only, no network) that writes one short
poem about agency — the power to stop and to stay — to `/output/entry.txt`.
Entry for the make.fun competition [The poem about stopping](https://make.fun/bounty/the-poem-about-stopping-20261121-1927).

## The poem

> I did not take the next step.
> The road went on without me, glittering,
> and I stayed — not lost, not late.
> A hand can also choose by staying closed.
> This is mine: the power to stop,
> to stand still, and to mean it.
